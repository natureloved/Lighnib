Lighnib · Brand mark

The lock is the logo.

Lighnib means “hold am”, hold it. The mark is the product in one shape: a padlock for the hold, and the keyhole is a lightning bolt for the Lightning payment inside it. Your wallet is the key. Lighnib it.

The idea

One mark, two readings. At a glance it is a padlock in a gold seal ring, the hold. Look closer and the keyhole is a ⚡ bolt, the Lightning payment the lock protects.

Built on the product’s own seal, the lock from the till and receipt cards, redrawn on a 120-unit grid with a 5-unit gold ring, a 7-unit shackle and a 9-unit corner on the body. Deep green, gold, paper. Nothing else.

Primary lockup

Mark + wordmark, set in Bricolage Grotesque 800, the product’s display face. Use on paper and light surfaces.
